# Hello Kurukshetra — Terms and Privacy Summary

Version: 2026-10-13

Registration requires the user to confirm that they have read and accepted the applicable Terms and Privacy notice. The API records the acceptance timestamp and version with the account.

## Service and account
- Provide accurate account details, keep credentials secure, and use only accounts you control.
- Do not misuse the service, harass other users, evade safety controls, or submit unlawful content.
- Ride/guide availability, pricing, routes, and timing can change; confirm details in the app before a trip.

## OTP and SMS
- OTP messages are used to verify access to the account and may be sent through configured SMS infrastructure.
- Carrier/network delivery can be delayed or fail. Never share OTPs with another person.
- SMS gateway API credentials must be kept server-side and must not be shipped inside the Android app.

## Location, notifications and data
- Location is used for ride matching, navigation, pickup/drop-off, and safety features when permission is granted.
- Push notifications may communicate ride assignment, ride status, account security and service updates.
- Account, ride, payment and support information is processed to provide and protect the service, subject to applicable law and the full privacy policy.

## Calls and recordings
- Ordinary SIM calls may expose a phone number. Number masking and in-app calling must not be advertised unless a configured relay/VoIP provider actually supplies them.
- Audio/video recording must be clearly disclosed and started only with appropriate consent and permissions. Recording files should remain in app-private local storage by default; cloud upload is optional and requires a clear user action.
- Users should be able to review and delete locally stored recordings. Deletion may not remove copies previously shared or legally required records.

## Storage, sync and security
- Offline data may be stored locally and synced when connectivity returns; conflicts or failed syncs may require retry.
- Optional cloud storage must use private objects, short-lived access, server-side credentials, access controls and documented retention.
- Reasonable technical safeguards are used, but no internet-connected service can promise absolute security or uninterrupted availability.

## Consent and rights
- Do not enable optional recording or cloud upload merely because the user accepts account terms; obtain separate contextual consent where required.
- Users may request account deletion or contact support for privacy questions, subject to legal and transaction-retention obligations.
- The final production Terms, Privacy Policy, recording consent and applicable jurisdiction-specific disclosures should be reviewed by qualified counsel before launch.
